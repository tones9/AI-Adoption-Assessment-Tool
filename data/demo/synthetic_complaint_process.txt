Customer Complaint Handling

The process handles a customer complaint from receipt through closure. Its objective is to record the issue, assess it, resolve it fairly, and communicate the outcome.

1. A customer service agent records the complaint and the customer's supporting information in the Case Management System.

2. The agent categorises the complaint and checks that required information is present. If information is missing, the agent asks the customer for clarification.

3. A complaints manager reviews the categorised complaint after the intake check is complete.

4. The manager decides whether specialist review is required. If specialist review is required, the case is assigned to a subject-matter specialist; otherwise it proceeds to resolution drafting.

5. The assigned reviewer drafts a proposed response in the Case Management System using the complaint record and applicable guidance.

6. The complaints manager approves or returns the proposed response. An exception occurs when the complaint indicates immediate customer harm, in which case the case is escalated before normal approval.

7. The agent sends the approved response to the customer and closes the case. The outputs are the customer response and the closed complaint record.
