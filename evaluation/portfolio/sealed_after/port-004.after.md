## Curated later reference evidence

### AI-assisted examiner prior-art search in PE2E

`PORT-004-S4` is the direct intervention source. The USPTO later reported that utility patent examiners began using its AI-assisted Similarity Search feature for prior-art searches in September 2022. The source describes application-text-based querying, ranked similar documents, examiner refinement controls, and retained examiner discretion.

`PORT-004-S2` supplies contemporaneous official context: AI search capabilities were being integrated into PE2E Search for examiners' prior-art searches, with use intended to be recorded in search history.

### Reported use context

`PORT-004-S3` reports that more than half of patent examiners regularly used PE2E AI search features and that the features had been used for more than 1.5 million queries since September 2022. This is an organisation-reported use figure. It is not a measured retrieval-quality, time-saving, or causal-impact result.

### Human-search boundary

`PORT-004-S4` states that the feature augments rather than replaces other search tools and that examiners retain discretion over using its results. `PORT-004-S5`, a PPAC advisory report, separately argues that AI search should remain supplementary to examiner judgement. The latter is advisory context, not evidence that every advised control was implemented.

## Source-quality limitations

- Three sources are USPTO-authored; they are authoritative for official tool description and reported use, but are not independent effectiveness evaluations.
- The PPAC report is advisory and does not independently measure the tool's impact.
- The sources describe a broader examiner search environment, not direct observation of the exact manual steps, time constraints, or outcomes in the frozen BEFORE corpus.
- No figures from different sources are merged, averaged, or treated as causal evidence.
