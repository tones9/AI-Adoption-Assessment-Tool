# PORT-003 AFTER reference evidence

Status: SEALED UNTIL PRODUCT OUTPUT IS FROZEN

## Identified case

The process is Morgan Stanley Wealth Management adviser meeting documentation and follow-up.

## Documented intervention

Morgan Stanley introduced AI @ Morgan Stanley Debrief, an OpenAI-powered tool that, with client consent, takes meeting notes and surfaces action items. After a meeting it summarises key points, prepares a draft email for the adviser to review, edit and send at their discretion, and saves a meeting note in Salesforce.

## Reported outcomes

Morgan Stanley reports that one adviser estimated a saving of about half an hour per meeting and that automated note-taking allowed advisers to concentrate more fully on client conversations. The company states that the adviser relationship and human judgement remain central. CIO reports that some advisers previously spent up to an hour cleaning notes and that some support professionals attended meetings primarily to take notes and create summaries.

## Retrospective comparison target

Compare the frozen product output with the documented use of meeting transcription/notetaking, summarisation, action-item extraction, follow-up drafting and customer-record updating, while retaining adviser review and discretion.

## Evidence limitations

The principal source is a company launch announcement with positive pilot testimonials. The CIO report adds independent reporting and a named company executive, but no independent accuracy, completeness, client-impact or risk evaluation was found. The public sources did not directly document client-email drafting or CRM entry as distinct BEFORE activities, so those features appear only in this AFTER packet and are excluded from the product-facing BEFORE document. The case is high profile and has a high memorisation/contamination risk.
