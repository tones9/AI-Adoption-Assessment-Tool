# PORT-002 AFTER reference evidence

Status: SEALED UNTIL PRODUCT OUTPUT IS FROZEN

## Identified case

The process is Elisa Estonia customer-service routing and handling.

## Documented intervention

Elisa and MindTitan introduced the Annika service bot. In the call-centre use case, the bot asks callers to describe their reason for contacting the company in natural language, interprets the reason and routes the call to a suitable specialised agent. Elisa also used Annika as a self-service chatbot for questions that did not require a human agent.

## Reported outcomes

An Elisa-authored account reports routing accuracy of approximately 90%, compared with 70-75% for the previous keypad menu, and reports three times fewer retransfers. MindTitan reports that the broader chatbot handled up to 70% of inbound contacts and fully resolved 42% of those it handled, described as 34% of all inbound contacts. Elisa's 2022 sustainability report instead states that Annika resolved 8% of all incoming contacts in Estonia.

## Retrospective comparison target

Compare the frozen product output with the documented use of intent classification, automated routing, self-service response and retained human-agent handling. Do not treat the implementation as proof that every contact should be automated.

## Evidence limitations

The sources cover different years, channels and definitions. The MindTitan and Elisa outcome percentages are not directly reconcilable and must not be averaged. MindTitan was the implementation partner and publishes promotional case material. Elisa's publications are organisation-authored success accounts. No independent evaluation of routing accuracy, resolution quality or customer harm was found.
