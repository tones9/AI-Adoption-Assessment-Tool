# PORT-001 AFTER reference evidence

Status: SEALED UNTIL PRODUCT OUTPUT IS FROZEN

## Identified case

An anonymised Nordic insurance firm worked with EY on claims-document processing.

## Documented intervention

Agents uploaded scanned claim documents to EY Fabric Document Intelligence. The implementation cleaned images, performed preprocessing and document/layout analysis, used optical character recognition and natural-language processing to extract and classify unstructured information, and transferred structured data to the insurer's core claims system. The implementation used controlled confidence levels and retained organisational control over automation.

## Reported outcomes

EY reports near-real-time claim-document processing and states that 70% of documents supplied to the system were correctly extracted and interpreted automatically. It also reports that agents had more time for customer interaction.

## Retrospective comparison target

Compare the frozen product output with the documented use of document information extraction, document classification and workflow transfer to the claims system. Do not treat implementation or reported outcomes as proof that this was the only suitable intervention or that it would be suitable in another organisation.

## Evidence limitations

The insurer is anonymous. EY is both implementation partner and publisher. No independent or insurer-authored corroboration was found. The publication does not disclose sample size, evaluation method, error distribution, implementation cost or negative outcomes. The 70% figure concerns documents correctly extracted and interpreted, not 70% of claims completed without human work.
