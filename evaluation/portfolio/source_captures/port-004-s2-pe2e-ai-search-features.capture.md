## Claim-level capture

The notice says the USPTO was moving AI search capabilities from beta versions into PE2E Search for the examining corps. Once tested and approved, the capabilities were to be available to patent examiners conducting prior-art searches during examination.

It also says the Office would record use of AI search capabilities in search history consistently with MPEP requirements. This supports a later, examiner-side intervention and a search-record traceability boundary.

## Scope and limitation

This is a contemporaneous official notice about implementation and recordation. It does not establish a deployment-wide adoption rate, a causal quality improvement, or that the frozen 2019/2020 workflow contained the later capability.
