## Locators and captured claims

- Section `A traditional approach to a modern challenge`: claim requests contain unstructured bills, invoices, pharmacy or clinic receipts, medical diagnoses and other supporting documents.
- Same section: agents manually analyse documents, extract required data and categorise information by relevance.
- Section describing the prior workflow: agents open individual images or PDF scans and upload relevant material to existing claims systems.
- Intervention section: scanned documents are processed using image cleaning, layout analysis, OCR and natural-language processing before structured data is transferred to the core claims system.
- Results section: EY reports near-real-time processing and 70% of supplied documents being correctly extracted and interpreted automatically.

## Boundary classification

- BEFORE: document inputs; manual opening, examination, extraction, categorisation and upload.
- AFTER: document-intelligence mechanism, confidence controls, system transfer and reported outcomes.

## Capture limitation

This is a non-verbatim claim-level research capture, not a complete copy of the copyrighted page. The source combines BEFORE and AFTER evidence in one retrospective success story.
