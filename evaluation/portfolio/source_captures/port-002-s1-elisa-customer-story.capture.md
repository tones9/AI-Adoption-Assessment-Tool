## Locators and captured claims

- Section `So, what does Annika do in our call center?`: customer-service topics are numerous and agents specialise because one person cannot be qualified to handle every topic.
- Same section: the earlier IVR plays menu recordings and customers choose language/topic routes with telephone number keys.
- Same section: with multiple menu layers, customers sometimes choose any option to reach an agent.
- Same section: 25-30% of choices were reported as incorrect; those contacts had to be retransferred by the receiving agent, increasing waiting and handling time.
- AFTER paragraphs: the voice bot asks the contact reason and routes the caller to an appropriate agent; reported routing accuracy and retransfer outcomes are recorded only in the sealed AFTER packet.

## Boundary classification

- BEFORE: topic diversity, specialist-agent structure, keypad IVR, wrong selections and receiving-agent retransfer.
- AFTER: natural-language intent routing and reported performance.

## Capture limitation

This is a non-verbatim claim-level research capture. It is organisation-authored and retrospective. The prior process does not directly describe a separate activity in which the receiving agent diagnoses the issue before retransfer; that earlier researcher inference has therefore been removed.
