## Claim-level capture

The report states that the USPTO provides AI-based search capabilities in an examiner patent-search tool and says they have assisted examiners in finding potential prior art early in examination.

The report also cautions that AI search should be supplementary to examiner expertise and judgement, and recommends careful parallel use rather than treating the tool as a substitute for human searching.

## Scope and limitation

PPAC is a statutory advisory body, so this source provides external-to-operating-team context but not independent measurement. Its caution is a recommendation and policy view; it is not evidence that the USPTO implemented each suggested control.
