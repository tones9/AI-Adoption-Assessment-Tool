Client-meeting documentation and follow-up planning

Purpose

This process creates an internal meeting record, summary and record of action items after an adviser meeting. The adviser remains responsible for the client relationship and decisions.

Current-state process

1. An adviser conducts a meeting with a client.
2. During the meeting, the adviser, a junior colleague or another team member records notes and action items manually.
3. After the meeting, the adviser or a support professional reviews and cleans up the notes.
4. The adviser or team prepares a meeting summary from the cleaned notes and recorded action items.

Known operating context

- Advisers may conduct several client meetings in a working day.
- Manual note-taking can divide attention during the meeting.
- Post-meeting documentation can require substantial adviser or support-team time.
- A support professional may attend a meeting primarily to take notes and prepare a summary.
- The adviser remains responsible for decisions and the client relationship.

Information not provided

- Meeting types, duration or distribution.
- Required note templates or mandatory record fields.
- How action owners and due dates are recorded or monitored.
- How action items are assigned, monitored or communicated.
- How the meeting record is entered into organisational systems.
- Detailed review, compliance or approval controls.
- Consequences of inaccurate or incomplete notes.
- Privacy, consent, security or retention requirements.
