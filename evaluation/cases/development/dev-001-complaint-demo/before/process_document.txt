Customer Complaint Handling Process

1. Receive complaint by email or web form.
2. Log the complaint in the CRM.
3. Classify the complaint by product and severity.
4. Investigate the complaint using order and account records.
5. Draft and send a response to the customer.
6. Close the complaint and record the outcome.

The source does not state volumes, timings, data quality, exception rates,
regulatory constraints, or error costs.
